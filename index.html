Add to your calendar

Best way: download the CSV and import it into a new calendar (e.g. "School") in Google Calendar or Outlook. Then you can toggle that calendar on or off on any device.

Re-importing after a change? Delete your old "School" calendar first, then import the new file into a fresh calendar. Otherwise you'll get duplicate classes.
